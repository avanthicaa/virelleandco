The Virelle Ritual

A luxurious mehendi experience crafted for brides and celebrations.

Step 1 — Prep Your Skin

Wash your hands thoroughly and avoid applying lotions or oils before mehendi. Clean skin helps achieve a darker stain.

Step 2 — Apply Mehendi

Use the premium Virelle cones to create elegant bridal or festive designs with smooth and effortless flow.

Step 3 — Apply Essential Oil

Dab a few drops of essential oil before and after drying to deepen the stain naturally and enhance longevity.

Step 4 — Use Sealant Spray

Spray lightly over dried mehendi to lock moisture and keep the paste intact for richer color development.

Step 5 — Mehendi Dip

Carefully apply the mehendi dip to intensify the final bridal stain and achieve a luxurious deep finish.

Step 6 — Aftercare Balm

Apply the balm after removing dried mehendi to nourish your skin and preserve stain richness beautifully.
